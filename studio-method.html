CALIBRATION METHOD / 01

From specification
to experience.

Our framework separates stated features, observed performance and personal preference so comparison remains useful.

THE OBSERVATION SEQUENCE

Five passes, one complete reference.

  1. 01

    Purpose

    Define the situations, information and comfort the watch needs to support.

  2. 02

    Proportion

    Compare diameter with lug span, thickness, case shape and bracelet articulation.

  3. 03

    Mechanism

    Read movement type, reserve, frequency, service access and setting behaviour together.

  4. 04

    Observation

    Record wear, timing and handling across a realistic interval rather than one moment.

  5. 05

    Ownership

    Plan service, storage and water-resistance checks without exaggerated permanence claims.
