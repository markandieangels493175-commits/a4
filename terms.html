Educational purpose

Chrono Prime Line provides general information about watches, specifications, ownership and care. It is not a guarantee that a product, method or service will suit every watch or wearer.

Specifications and variation

Specifications may change by reference, production period and region. Confirm current manufacturer information before purchasing or servicing. Images show editorial reference objects and do not promise an identical available item.

Responsible practice

Follow the watch manufacturer’s manual and safety guidance. Do not open cases, force controls or improvise water-resistance tests. Qualified watchmakers should assess internal faults, seal condition and structural concerns.
